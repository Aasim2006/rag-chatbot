NimbusTech Employee Handbook (Sample)

1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST, with a one-hour lunch break. Employees may request flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM after discussion with their manager.

2. Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 18 paid leave days per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Unused leave up to 5 days can be carried forward to the next year.

3. Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require HR sign-off and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis depending on role and team needs.

4. Reimbursements
Internet and mobile reimbursements up to INR 1,500 per month are available for employees who work from home regularly. Travel for official work purposes is reimbursed at actual cost with prior approval and valid receipts.

5. Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October. Ratings are based on goal completion, peer feedback, and manager evaluation. Promotions are typically considered during the October review cycle.

6. Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to maintain professionalism, respect confidentiality of company and client data, and avoid conflicts of interest. Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited and can be reported confidentially to HR.

7. Exit Policy
Employees resigning from the company are required to serve a notice period of 60 days for full-time roles and 15 days for internships. Exit interviews are conducted by HR before the final settlement is processed.
