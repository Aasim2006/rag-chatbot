NimbusCloud Product FAQ (Sample)

What is NimbusCloud?
NimbusCloud is a cloud storage and file-sharing platform designed for small and medium businesses. It offers automatic backup, version history, and team collaboration tools.

What are the pricing plans?
NimbusCloud offers three plans: Starter (100 GB, INR 299/month), Team (1 TB, INR 999/month), and Business (5 TB, INR 2,999/month). All plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

How does file versioning work?
Every time a file is edited, NimbusCloud automatically saves a new version. Users can view and restore up to 30 previous versions of any file for free. Business plan users get unlimited version history.

Is my data encrypted?
Yes. All files are encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption. NimbusCloud is also compliant with GDPR and SOC 2 Type II standards.

Can I share files with people outside my organization?
Yes, you can generate shareable links with optional password protection and expiry dates. External viewers do not need a NimbusCloud account to view shared files.

What happens if I exceed my storage limit?
You will receive an email notification when you reach 90% of your storage limit. Uploads are paused once you exceed 100% of your plan's storage until you either delete files or upgrade your plan.

How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel anytime from Account Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription. Your data remains accessible in read-only mode for 30 days after cancellation before being permanently deleted.
